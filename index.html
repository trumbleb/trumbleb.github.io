[EDIT ME — 2–3 sentence project abstract. Suggested starting point, adapt freely:]

My dissertation, "After Much Discussion": Participation, Trust, and Boredom in the New England Town Meeting, examines the institution of New England Town Meeting — a form of participatory, deliberative, actually existing local democracy — through a self-created longitudinal dataset of over 8,000 warrant articles (1956–2025) and ethnographic observation of 20 town meetings (2025–26).
